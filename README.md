# Hi, I'm Joseph C. Henry 👋

**AI & Cloud Engineer** at Dell Technologies, building the core platform behind **MTN Cloud**. BEng in Electrical & Electronics Engineering, Covenant University.

I build across cloud infrastructure, backend systems, and applied AI, from OpenStack automation to LLM-powered products and robots with web interfaces.

## 🔧 What I Do

- **Cloud & infrastructure:** OpenStack, Morpheus-based automation, managed cloud services (e.g. Managed Kafka), and client migrations
- **Custom cloud management platform:** building a CMP on top of OpenStack, backed by a multi-node lab
- **Backend & APIs:** REST, WebSockets, GraphQL, and full-stack web apps
- **AI & ML:** NLP, computer vision, model training and deployment, AI-integrated robotics
- **Embedded & robotics:** Arduino, Raspberry Pi, control systems

## 🌱 Currently

- Building and scaling MTN Cloud's platform
- Preparing for the AWS Solutions Architect Associate exam
- Exploring control engineering, aerospace, and algorithms
